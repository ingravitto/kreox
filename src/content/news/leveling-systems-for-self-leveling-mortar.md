**A leveling system for self-leveling mortar is a set of tools that precisely control the height, thickness and spread of the mortar while a floor is installed, so the surface comes out perfectly level.** KREOX makes two of them in Spain: [PIKO](/en/self-leveling-mortar-levelers/piko/) for underfloor heating and the [Nivelador Universal](/en/self-leveling-mortar-levelers/universal-leveler/) for screeds.

Applying **self-leveling mortar** has become one of the most widely used solutions in modern construction to achieve perfectly level surfaces. However, to achieve **precise, even leveling** it is essential to use specific tools that control the height and thickness of the material during installation.

**Leveling systems for self-leveling mortar** make work on site more precise, faster and safer: they guarantee an even surface, reduce errors during application and optimize material use.

## What a leveling system for self-leveling mortar is

They are tools designed to precisely control the height, thickness and spread of the mortar during floor installation. They are used especially in:

- **Underfloor heating** installations
- Floor screeds
- Leveling surfaces before applying floor finishes
- Technical and industrial floors
- Refurbishment and new-build projects

## Why use professional levelers

Self-leveling mortar flows and levels naturally, but without a control system the thickness can vary or irregularities can appear. Levelers bring:

- **More precision:** the height of the mortar is set exactly.
- **More speed:** clear reference points during pouring.
- **Less wasted material:** no excess or shortfall of mortar.
- **Professional results:** an even surface, key in technical floors and underfloor heating.
