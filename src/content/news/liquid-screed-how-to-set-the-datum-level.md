**To mark the datum level of a liquid screed, you rest levellers on the substrate, roughly one every 2 m², mark the datum height with a laser, adjust the ring of each leveller to that line and pour the screed up to the ring.** On a slab, subfloor or existing floor, where nothing can be pushed in, you use the [KREOX Nivelador Universal](/en/screed-levellers/universal-screed-leveller/), which rests on its flat base.

## What you need

- **[Nivelador Universal levellers](/en/screed-levellers/universal-screed-leveller/)**: boxes of 40 units.
- **A laser level**: standard or with receiver.
- **Liquid screed**, following its manufacturer’s instructions.

## Step 1 · Set the levellers down

Place the Nivelador Universal levellers on the surface to be screeded, roughly **one every 2 m²**. Their flat, reinforced base keeps them stable.

## Step 2 · Mark the level with a laser

Use the laser to mark the datum height of the screed.

## Step 3 · Adjust the rings

Slide the ring on each leveller up to the laser line.

## Step 4 · Pour and remove

Pour the liquid screed up to the height of the rings. Then remove the levellers and clean them for the next job.

## Universal or PIKO?

- **Hard surface** (slab, subfloor, existing floor) → [Nivelador Universal](/en/screed-levellers/universal-screed-leveller/), which rests on the surface.
- **Underfloor heating with insulation board** → [PIKO](/en/screed-levellers/piko/), which is pushed in.

## Spare parts

If a part wears out, you replace only that part: universal levelling base, metal ring or washer. You can see them in [spare parts](/en/screed-levellers/spare-parts/).
