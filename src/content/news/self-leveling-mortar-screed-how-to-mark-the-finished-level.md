**To mark the finished level of a self-leveling mortar screed, you rest levelers on the substrate, roughly one every 2 m², mark the finished height with a laser, adjust the ring of each leveler to that line and pour the mortar up to the ring.** On a slab, subfloor or existing floor, where nothing can be pushed in, you use the [KREOX Nivelador Universal](/en/self-leveling-mortar-levelers/universal-leveler/), which rests on its flat base.

## What you need

- **[Nivelador Universal levelers](/en/self-leveling-mortar-levelers/universal-leveler/)**: boxes of 40 units.
- **A laser level**: standard or with receiver.
- **Self-leveling mortar**, following its manufacturer’s instructions.

## Step 1 · Set the levelers down

Place the Nivelador Universal levelers on the surface to be screeded, roughly **one every 2 m²**. Their flat, reinforced base keeps them stable.

## Step 2 · Mark the level with a laser

Use the laser to mark the finished height of the screed.

## Step 3 · Adjust the rings

Slide the ring on each leveler up to the laser line.

## Step 4 · Pour and remove

Pour the self-leveling mortar up to the height of the rings. Then remove the levelers and clean them for the next job.

## Universal or PIKO?

- **Hard surface** (slab, subfloor, existing floor) → [Nivelador Universal](/en/self-leveling-mortar-levelers/universal-leveler/), which rests on the surface.
- **Underfloor heating with insulation board** → [PIKO](/en/self-leveling-mortar-levelers/piko/), which is pushed in.

## Spare parts

If a part wears out, you replace only that part: universal leveling base, metal ring or washer. You can see them in [spare parts](/en/self-leveling-mortar-levelers/spare-parts/).
