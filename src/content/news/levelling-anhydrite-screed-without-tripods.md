**To level anhydrite screed without tripods, you place plastic screed levellers at roughly one per m², mark the finished datum with a laser, set the ring of each leveller to that line and pump the anhydrite up to the height of the rings.** Over underfloor heating you use the [Insulation Screed Leveller](/en/screed-levellers/insulation-screed-leveller/), pushed into the insulation board; on a slab, subfloor or existing floor, the [Universal Screed Leveller](/en/screed-levellers/universal-screed-leveller/), which rests on its flat base.

## Anhydrite and screed levellers: what changes and what does not

Anhydrite, or calcium sulphate, is a very fluid liquid screed that is widely used over underfloor heating and for screeds on slabs. It is pumped quickly over large areas, so the level points have to be in place and set before the pour starts, and they must not get in the way while the applicator moves along with the hose.

KREOX screed levellers set the datum in exactly the same way with anhydrite as with cement-based liquid screed. What changes compared with metal tripods is the weight and the setting out: there are no three legs to seat at every point.

## Step by step

### 1 · Place the levellers

Spread them over the whole area, roughly **one per m²** on a 1 m grid. Over underfloor heating, the insulation leveller is pushed into the insulation board without tools. On a slab, the universal leveller simply rests on the substrate.

### 2 · Mark the datum with the laser

Set the laser on its tripod and mark the finished height. A standard laser or a laser with a receiver both work.

### 3 · Set the ring of each leveller

Move the ring up or down to the laser line. That ring is the reference the applicator sees during the pour.

### 4 · Pump the anhydrite up to the ring

The anhydrite flows across the room and settles at the height of the rings. When the job is finished, the levellers are cleaned with water and reused on the next one.

## How many levellers do I need?

| Area | Levellers (1 per m²) | Boxes of insulation levellers (100) | Boxes of universal levellers (40) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 m² | 50 | 1 | 2 |
| 100 m² | 100 | 1 | 3 |
| 200 m² | 200 | 2 | 5 |
| 500 m² | 500 | 5 | 13 |

If you have several areas or floors, the [leveller calculator](/en/screed-levellers/calculator/) gives you units, boxes and an estimate of screed area by area.

## Before you pump

The leveller sets the datum; it does not change how the substrate is prepared. For depths, perimeter strip, joints and drying times, always follow the anhydrite manufacturer's data sheet.
